DARVOZ Privacy Policy

One App. Every Need.

Welcome to DARVOZ. This Privacy Policy explains how DARVOZ collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, application, and local delivery services.

By using DARVOZ, you acknowledge and agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use DARVOZ, we may collect information such as:

  • Name.
  • Mobile number.
  • Email address, if provided.
  • Delivery address.
  • Location information required for delivery.
  • Order and transaction details.
  • Account and login information.
  • Information provided when contacting support.

We aim to collect only information reasonably required to provide and operate our local services.

2. OTP and Account Verification

DARVOZ may use OTP verification to verify your mobile number and help secure your account.

OTPs may be sent through SMS, WhatsApp, or other authorized communication services.

You should never share your OTP with another person.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process and deliver orders.
  • Connect customers with local Partners.
  • Connect orders with Delivery Partners.
  • Verify your mobile number.
  • Process payments and refunds.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send order and delivery updates.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse.
  • Improve DARVOZ services.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

4. Location Information

DARVOZ may request location information to help provide local delivery services and determine whether services are available in your area.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work correctly if required location access is disabled.

5. Sharing Information

We may share information that is necessary to provide our services with:

  • Restaurants and local stores handling your order.
  • Delivery Partners handling your delivery.
  • Payment service providers.
  • OTP and communication service providers.
  • Technology and hosting service providers.
  • Authorities when required by applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information simply for the purpose of selling it to third parties.

6. Payments

Payments may be processed through authorized third-party payment providers.

DARVOZ may receive transaction information necessary to confirm and manage your payment.

Payment providers may process payment information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Cookies

DARVOZ may use cookies and similar technologies to help:

  • Keep users signed in.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Improve website functionality.
  • Improve security.
  • Understand how our Platform is used.

You may control cookies through your browser settings where supported.

8. Data Security

DARVOZ takes reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are also responsible for keeping your account credentials and OTPs confidential.

9. Data Retention

DARVOZ may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain order records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of, subject to applicable requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

DARVOZ services are not intended for children where use would violate applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by law.

11. Third-Party Services

DARVOZ may use third-party services such as payment gateways, OTP providers, maps, hosting services, and other technology providers.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

You may contact DARVOZ regarding privacy-related requests or concerns.

13. Account Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your DARVOZ account or personal information, you may contact DARVOZ through the support or contact details available on our Platform.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

14. Communications

DARVOZ may send service-related communications such as account verification, order updates, delivery updates, support messages, and other necessary notifications.

Promotional communications, where applicable, will be handled according to applicable requirements and available opt-out options.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

DARVOZ may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updated versions will be published on the DARVOZ Platform with a revised Last Updated date.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions, complaints, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact DARVOZ through the support or contact details available on our Platform.
