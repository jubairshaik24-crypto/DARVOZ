DARVOZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

DARVOZ respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how DARVOZ collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website, application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and contact information, including phone number and email address.
  • Delivery addresses and location information required to process orders.
  • Order, payment and transaction information.
  • Account information provided when registering with DARVOZ.
  • Information required to communicate with you through WhatsApp or other supported communication services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your DARVOZ account.
  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Send order confirmations, updates and customer support messages.
  • Send authentication codes and account verification messages.
  • Improve the DARVOZ platform and customer experience.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.

3. WhatsApp Communication

If you provide your phone number and choose to communicate with DARVOZ through WhatsApp, we may use WhatsApp Business services to send authentication codes, order updates, delivery notifications and customer support messages.

You can manage or stop certain communications according to the options available in WhatsApp or by contacting DARVOZ support.

4. Sharing of Information

DARVOZ may share necessary information with restaurants, stores, delivery partners and service providers only when required to process and complete your order or provide our services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. DARVOZ does not intentionally store sensitive card information unless required by an authorized payment service and applicable law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

DARVOZ may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
