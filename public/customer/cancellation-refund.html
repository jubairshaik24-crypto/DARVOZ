DARVOZ Cancellation & Refund Policy

This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains when a customer may cancel an order and when a refund may be provided through DARVOZ.

This policy applies to the current local services provided by DARVOZ.

1. Order Cancellation by Customer

Customers may request cancellation of an order through the DARVOZ Platform, where the cancellation option is available.

Cancellation is generally possible before the Partner begins preparing or processing the order.

Important: Once an order has been prepared, packed, dispatched, or handed to a Delivery Partner, cancellation may not be possible.

2. Cancellation After Order Preparation

If a customer requests cancellation after preparation has started, DARVOZ may not be able to provide a refund because the Partner may already have incurred costs for the order.

Any refund in such circumstances will be considered based on the individual situation.

3. Cancellation by DARVOZ or Partner

DARVOZ or the relevant Partner may cancel an order when reasonably necessary.

This may happen because of:

  • Product or item unavailability.
  • Partner unable to accept or prepare the order.
  • Delivery unavailable in the customer's area.
  • Payment failure.
  • Incorrect or incomplete customer information.
  • Technical problems.
  • Suspected fraudulent activity.
  • Other circumstances beyond reasonable control.

4. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in situations such as:

  • An eligible order is cancelled before preparation.
  • Payment was successfully charged but the order could not be completed.
  • An order contains missing or incorrect items and the issue is verified.
  • The order was cancelled by DARVOZ or the Partner for reasons that qualify for a refund.
  • Other genuine order-related issues reviewed by DARVOZ.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may not be provided when an issue occurs because of the customer's actions, including:

  • Providing an incorrect delivery address.
  • Providing incorrect contact information.
  • Being unavailable to receive the order.
  • Refusing to accept a correctly delivered order.
  • Providing false or misleading information.
  • Misusing cancellation or refund services.

6. Missing or Incorrect Items

If your order contains a missing or incorrect item, please contact DARVOZ support as soon as possible after receiving the order.

DARVOZ may review the issue with the relevant Partner and determine an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, partial refund, full refund, or another solution where appropriate.

7. Damaged or Poor-Quality Items

If an item arrives damaged or there is a genuine quality issue, customers should report the issue promptly through DARVOZ support.

DARVOZ may request relevant information, such as photographs or order details, to review the complaint.

8. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method where possible.

The time taken for the refund to appear in your account may depend on the bank, UPI provider, payment gateway, or other payment service.

9. Cash Payments

Where cash payment is available, refund handling may be different from online payments.

DARVOZ may provide an appropriate refund method depending on the circumstances and available payment options.

10. Refund Requests

Customers should contact DARVOZ support as soon as possible when requesting a refund.

Please provide your order details and a clear explanation of the issue so that DARVOZ can review the request.

11. Fraudulent Refund Claims

DARVOZ may investigate refund requests to prevent fraudulent or repeated misuse of the refund system.

Accounts involved in repeated fraudulent refund claims, false complaints, or other misuse may be restricted or suspended.

12. Changes to This Policy

DARVOZ may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time.

Any updated version will be published on the DARVOZ Platform with a revised Last Updated date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding cancellations or refunds, please contact DARVOZ through the support or contact details available on our Platform.
